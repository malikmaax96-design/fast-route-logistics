1. Information we collect

If you apply to drive with us

  • Your name, phone number and email address.
  • Your answers to our application questions, such as whether you hold a full UK driving licence, whether you have a van, your nearest depot and when you can start.
  • If your application progresses: driving licence details, proof of right to work in the UK, the result of a Disclosure and Barring Service (DBS) check, vehicle and insurance details, and the payment details we need to pay you.

If you contact us or use our services

  • Your name and contact details, and the content of your message.
  • Delivery information such as addresses and parcel tracking details, where we deliver to you on behalf of a retailer or carrier.

If you visit our website

  • Basic technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you view. See Cookies.

We collect most of this directly from you. We do not buy contact lists.

3. Applications through Facebook and Instagram

When you submit one of our application forms on Facebook or Instagram, Meta Platforms passes us the details you entered or confirmed in that form. We use those details only to contact you about driving work with FRL Couriers. Meta's own use of your information is covered by Meta's Privacy Policy.

4. Who we share it with

We share personal information only where it is needed, and only with:

  • Service providers who host our systems, email and recruitment records, acting on our instructions.
  • The Disclosure and Barring Service and the registered body that processes DBS checks for us.
  • The delivery networks and clients whose parcels we carry, where they need to know which driver is delivering on their behalf.
  • Our accountants, insurers and professional advisers.
  • HMRC, the Home Office, the police or courts where the law requires it.

5. International transfers

Some of our service providers may store information outside the UK. Where that happens, we make sure it is protected by UK adequacy regulations or by contractual safeguards approved under UK law, such as the International Data Transfer Agreement.

6. How long we keep it

  • Applications that do not go ahead: 6 months from our last contact with you, then deleted.
  • Drivers who work with us: for as long as you work with us and 6 years afterwards, to meet tax and legal requirements.
  • Right-to-work evidence: 2 years after you stop working with us.
  • DBS results: we record the outcome and date only and do not keep the certificate itself longer than 6 months.
  • General enquiries: up to 12 months.

7. How we protect it

We limit access to staff who need it, use password-protected systems with encryption in transit, and require our service providers to apply appropriate security. If a breach ever puts your rights at risk, we will tell you and the Information Commissioner's Office as the law requires.

8. Cookies

Our website uses cookies that are strictly necessary for it to work. If we use analytics or advertising cookies, such as those from Google or Meta, we will ask for your consent first, and you can change your choice at any time. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

9. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the information we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct information that is wrong or incomplete;
  • ask us to delete your information, or to restrict how we use it;
  • object to us using your information on the basis of legitimate interests;
  • ask us to transfer your information to you or another organisation;
  • withdraw consent at any time where we rely on consent.

To use any of these rights, email info@frlcouriers.co.uk. We will reply within one month and there is normally no charge.

10. Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, please contact us first so we can put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of the page shows when it was last changed. If a change significantly affects you, we will let you know directly where we can.
